# GARE — Promo Video: Timing Sheet & Locked VO Script

Two-part short-form series (vertical 9:16). Part 2 shows the pains people
recognize; Part 3 reveals the app as the answer to each pain.

- **Total runtime:** Part 2 ≈ 1:26 · Part 3 ≈ 1:28
- **Read pace:** calm documentary, one idea per beat. Do not rush the VO.
- **Timecodes are cumulative within each part.**

## Global rules

- Cinematic documentary grade, natural window light, shallow depth of field.
- Real Rwandan faces and places; natural acting, no exaggerated expressions.
- App UI shown crisp and unblurred; no readable third-party text; no logos.
- Same colour grade across both parts.
- Music: sparse; one rising theme into the Part 3 reveal; cut to silence on the
  logo card.

## Locked VO script

### Part 2

**05 — The salon owner**
> Meet Aline. Great service, loyal clients, a spotless salon — and a dozen things to manage at once. The work is good. The watching never stops.

**06 — The hardware shop owner**
> His shop is full. But search for him online — nothing. The customers he never meets don't even know he exists.

**07 — The restaurant owner**
> The food is loved. But every quiet Tuesday, the owner starts from zero. Loyalty that lives only in people's heads disappears.

**08 — The boutique owner**
> Thousands follow her. Hundreds message her. She answers all day — and still loses the sale. Attention is not a business. Not yet.

**09 — The garage mechanic**
> Some weeks the bay is full. Some weeks it's silent. He has no idea where the next job is coming from. Feast, then famine.

**10 — The pharmacy / clinic desk**
> A paper book. People who were supposed to come back. Half never get called. They don't leave angry — they quietly forget.

**11 — The cyber café / printing shop**
> Too much work sounds good — until he's turning customers away. The jobs he rejects go to someone else, and never come back.

**12 — The training center owner**
> Every student came from someone else. He has no idea who sent them, or how to get more. His future rides on luck and memory.

**13 — The young freelancer**
> He's good. Fast. Cheap. And invisible. Talent isn't the problem — finding the next client is.

**14 — The turn**
> *(no voice-over)*

### Part 3

**15 — The turn-in**
> Remember them? Different businesses — the same blind spot. So we built the thing that does the watching.

**16 — Radar**
> No more guessing where the next job is. GARE scans your market and shows the work that's actually out there.

**17 — Revenue Leaks**
> It reads a business's public presence and finds the exact gaps you can fix — with evidence, not a guess.

**18 — Offer Studio + Proposals**
> Every gap maps to an offer. Every offer has a delivery plan. One tap turns it into a message you can send.

**19 — Recurring**
> And the work that should repeat? GARE tracks it — what's active, what renews, what's overdue.

**20 — Updates**
> You stop watching everything. The daily scan runs itself and drops what changed into one feed.

**21 — Partners**
> Too much work is no longer a loss. Route what you can't deliver to partners you trust — and keep your cut.

**22 — Recovery**
> The leads that went quiet don't get forgotten. GARE finds the stale ones and hands you the next step.

**23 — Referrals**
> And the word-of-mouth is finally a list — who referred who, and who to ask next.

**24 — Buyer Intent**
> It doesn't invent anything. It shows the truth of your pipeline — who's warm, who's cold, and exactly why.

**25 — The close**
> One engine. Find the gaps. Fix them. Track what repeats — and ask for the next one. GARE. Stop guessing where the next customer is.
